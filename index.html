About my process

My vision, built with AI tools.

I came up with the vision for ModCut and guided its development with Claude Code and another AI coding agent. My role was to decide what to build, explain it clearly, and keep testing until the result matched what I had in mind.

  1. 1

    Shape the vision

    Decide what ModCut should be and how each feature should feel to use.

  2. 2

    Write prompts

    Describe features to the AI tools, often with examples from finished edits.

  3. 3

    Test the results

    Run what comes back, compare it with the goal, and find what's wrong.

  4. 4

    Give feedback

    Explain what to change and repeat until it works the way I intended.

Transitions from real edits

I used examples from finished edits to show the AI the kind of transitions I wanted, then guided it toward recreating similar transitions in ModCut.

A pre-existing AI model, guided by me

ModCut includes a downloadable, pre-existing AI model. I didn't train it from scratch. I guided how it's set up and how it behaves through prompts, examples, testing and feedback.

How it started

About three years ago, when I was 13, I was learning Unity. My first game followed a tutorial. Later I started using ChatGPT to help write code for my own projects, and the code often had bugs or didn't do what I expected.

So I learned through trial and error: how to explain my ideas clearly, test AI-generated code, find the problems, and guide the AI toward what I wanted. Sometimes I thought ideas through while walking my dog. That's the same process I used to build ModCut.
